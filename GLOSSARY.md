# Asia Visual Grafika Chatbot

Terms used when discussing the business chatbot, its administration, and the thesis evaluation.

## Language

**Customer conversation**:
A customer's exchange of messages with the business chatbot, including follow-up questions that depend on earlier messages.

**Knowledge base**:
The business information maintained for the chatbot to answer questions about Asia Visual Grafika's services, requirements, and policies.
_Avoid_: Knowledge database when referring to the business information itself.

**Admin dashboard**:
The business operator's workspace for maintaining chatbot knowledge, inspecting conversations, configuring the chatbot, and reviewing evaluations.

**Playground**:
The administrator's chat interface for trying the chatbot and inspecting the evidence behind its answers.

**Evaluation baseline**:
A recorded measurement of the existing chatbot before the agreed refactor, retained as a reference for later comparisons in the thesis.

**Evaluation run**:
One execution of a defined set of chatbot evaluation cases under a recorded configuration.

**Draft knowledge version**:
A candidate set of business information that an administrator can inspect and test before making it available to customers.

**Published knowledge version**:
The set of business information selected by the administrator for answering customer conversations.

**Reference answer**:
A reviewed answer that describes the business facts an evaluation case expects the chatbot to communicate.

**Evaluation scenario**:
A customer question or conversation with a defined expected answer or outcome used to assess the chatbot.
