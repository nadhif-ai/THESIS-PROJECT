## Best practices checked through Exa MCP

DeepEval Contextual Recall attributes each expected-output statement to retrieved passages, then divides attributable statements by all reference statements. The generated answer is not a scoring dependency in the installed implementation. Diagnose rejected reference statements individually: improving answer prose cannot recover missing retrieval evidence. [Official documentation](https://deepeval.com/docs/metrics-contextual-recall), [official implementation](https://github.com/confident-ai/deepeval/blob/main/deepeval/metrics/contextual_recall/contextual_recall.py).

Separate absent corpus facts, missing initial candidates, reranking omissions and judge attribution errors. Pinecone distinguishes candidate depth from reranked output depth: reranking cannot recover passages excluded earlier. This application's lexical scores are computed within the dense candidate pool, not through an independent corpus-wide BM25 search. Increase depth only when measured retrieval evidence supports the resulting precision/recall tradeoff. Preserve context that makes a passage understandable when split from its source. [Pinecone reranking guidance](https://sdk.pinecone.io/python/how-to/inference/reranking.html), [Anthropic contextual retrieval](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/contextual-retrieval).

Reasoning tokens count toward output budgets. A completion can consume its budget before emitting parseable JSON. Hiding reasoning is not equivalent to disabling it; supported efforts must be verified per model. Exa research, Context7 and live provider model discovery agree on this contract. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash supports `none`; GPT-OSS 20B and GLM 5.3 Flash require reasoning and support `low`. [OpenRouter reasoning guidance](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/best-practices/reasoning-tokens).

Freeze references, preserve unsuccessful runs, record source/model/prompt/judge changes and report uncertainty. A ten-case development result is not a probability that customer answers are correct. The DP reference conflict is reported as a limitation rather than copying an unsupported 50% minimum into business knowledge. With that case at zero, this ten-case mean is capped at 90% even if all remaining cases are perfect. [Evaluation statistics](https://www.anthropic.com/research/statistical-approach-to-model-evals).
